### 13:00 · surprise · Accessibility writes steal the foreground window

Two windows driven in parallel had no crossed values and no errors, but `click` and `set-value` took the
foreground window even in headless mode. The engine had assumed accessibility actions need no physical
input. The person chose to serialise every acting node.

**Why it matters:** "no mouse" did not mean "not in the person's way".
**Evidence:** commits `c99fb02`, `5873dfb` · `docs/research/20-parallel-accessibility-actions.md`
**In their words:** "opción 1"

### 15:00 · problem · A live test overwrote text in the person's Notepad
